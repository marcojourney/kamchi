Privacy and security

Your borrowers' data stays on your phone. KamChi never sees it.

As a lender, you handle sensitive names, ID photos, and payment records. KamChi is built so your borrower records stay on your phone—not on our servers.

Stored locally

On your device

  • Borrower names and phone numbers
  • National ID photos
  • Loan amounts and repayment schedules
  • Payment records and receipts
Never uploaded
License check only

On KamChi's server

  • Your license key
  • Your plan
  • Expiry date
  • Device ID

We never hold borrower data

Names, ID photos, and loan amounts stay on your phone and are never uploaded to KamChi.

Lock it down

Protect the app with a PIN or your phone's fingerprint or face lock before anyone can open it.

Works offline

After activating your license once, you do not need internet to add loans or record payments.

You stay in control

Export your records any time, even if your license expires and the app is in read-only mode.

You are in charge of your backups

Backups are encrypted and saved where you choose—as a file on your device, in your own cloud drive, or in your Telegram. A lost phone without a backup means lost records, so back up often.

What we do collect

  • Your license key
  • Your plan and expiry date
  • Your device ID
  • The email, phone number, or Telegram handle you give us when you buy

Nothing else.
