Why switch from paper

Stop losing track of payments in notebooks and spreadsheets

Personal lending runs on trust and accuracy. One missed note or spreadsheet formula error can cost you money and strain relationships.

Before

Paper notebooks & Excel sheets

  • Hard to tell who is overdue without flipping through pages
  • Manual interest calculations take time and cause mistakes
  • USD and KHR balances mixed together in separate columns
  • Borrower ID photos lost in chat threads with no backup
With KamChi

One calm, organized app

  • See today’s collections and overdue borrowers on one screen
  • Automatic schedules for flat, reducing balance, or interest-only
  • Clear separate totals for USD and KHR with KHQR receipt logs
  • ID photos, guarantors, and encrypted backups safely in your pocket

Built for real lending workflows

Everything you need to lend clearly
